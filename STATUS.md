# Status — Anaesthesia Training Companion (novice-anaesthetist-syllabus)

_Last updated: 2026-09-30_

**Lifecycle stage:** active — full draft built, awaiting clinical QC

## Where things stand
The site now covers **all stages of anaesthetic training** (decision 2026-09-27). It is live as a draft at **https://fruitbat3000.github.io/anaesthesia-training-companion/** (repo https://github.com/fruitbat3000/anaesthesia-training-companion, renamed from novice-anaesthetist-syllabus on 2026-09-28; the old Pages address no longer works). The site is marked `noindex`. Contents:

- **Stage pages:** Novice (the original IAC section), Stage 1, Stage 2, Stage 3, built from the RCoA stage pages, curriculum v1.5 guidance and the National Anaesthetic ARCP Checklist v4.0. Each stage has its own colour, an expanding sidebar menu with progress, and:
  - **Key capabilities:** every RCoA key capability for the stage (147 / 139 / 133), paraphrased from the v1.5 learning syllabus with letters and supervision levels, tickable, with evidence ideas and links to notes, stations and e-LA modules; plus a printable checklist.
  - **Unit guides:** 7 for Stage 1 and 13 for Stage 2 (including the four Triple C areas), each embedding the relevant capabilities as live tick boxes.
  - **Special Interest Areas:** all 26 SIAs (179 capabilities), with the rules for the SIA year.
- **Novice section:** sidebar subheadings and progress, per-domain progress, and "Go further" links from 74 novice topics to Primary notes and questions.
- **Exams hub (7 pages):** the July 2027 changes (Primary AKT + CASE, Final AKT + FCPE), transition rules, Primary and Final details, study plan, a question bank directory, and a trainers' page based on the RCoA Trainer Support Pack (June 2026).
- **Syllabus overview (`#map`):** the four-box syllabus from the source novice guide (1 Basic sciences, 2 Medicine and surgery, 3 Generic anaesthesia, 4 Anaesthetic specialities), laid out as in the original diagram, with every novice topic, Primary and Final note, unit guide and SIA linked from its box and a filter for Novice / Primary / Final. Linked from the home page, the Revision notes menu and the Novice, Stage 1 and Stage 2 menus.
- **Syllabus maps:** readable Primary and Final maps (by paper and subject, or by RCoA section) with red/amber/green self-rating, a "revise next" list, verified core textbooks (12, with current editions) and free resources per subject.
- **Revision notes:** 131 Primary notes covering **all 610** Primary syllabus codes (v2.2) and 60 Final notes covering **all 436** Stage 2 codes, each with references and an "In the exam" box (AKT plus a CASE/FCPE prompt), plus a code coverage map. 144 notes carry hand-picked BJA Education articles (197 in total).
- **Question bank:** 249 Primary and 124 Final original SBAs with per-option explanations; practice by topic, timed mock papers built to the AKT blueprint (Primary Paper A, Paper B, Final AKT — all can run at full length), results by topic, and progress tracking.
- **Station practice:** 21 packs (11 CASE, 10 FCPE) with candidate and examiner views, a 2 + 9 minute timer, a key-feature checklist, domain and global ratings using the RCoA marking language, and a copyable feedback summary.
- **Resources:** guidelines library (including all current GPAS chapters, also linked from unit guides and SIAs), portfolio and ARCP, wellbeing and flexibility, specialist society links.
- **Disclaimer** page and a site-wide banner (educational only, not medical advice, no liability, check doses against the BNF).
- 81 references, 12 core textbooks and 197 BJA Education DOIs, all verified. The weekly link check covers every content link (last run 2026-09-28: 394 OK, 0 problems; RCoA, Cambridge, Oxford Academic and other bot-protected links listed for manual checks). The deploy workflow validates and builds the content.

**Nothing has been clinically reviewed yet.** Every note, question and station shows a "Draft" badge.
