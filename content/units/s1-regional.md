## What the block is like

Much regional experience comes on ordinary lists (orthopaedics, urology, obstetrics), so ask your rota coordinator for lists where blocks are routine. Joint RA-UK and RCoA guidance for trainers sets out how the regional outcomes can be achieved.

## What you need to show

{{caps stage-1 ra A,B,C,D,E,F,J,K}}

## Cases to seek out

- Spinals for joint replacement, TURP and caesarean section.
- Ultrasound-guided fascia iliaca or femoral blocks for hip fracture, often in the emergency department.
- Wrist and simple upper limb blocks.

## Getting the evidence

- DOPS for each technique, repeated as your supervision level changes.
- Evidence of "Stop Before You Block" in every SLE.
- Part-task trainers and ultrasound courses count as personal activities.

> [!warn] Safety first
> Know where the lipid emulsion is and how to use it before your first block. Rehearse local anaesthetic toxicity as a team.
