## Where to get it

- Trauma calls and damage-control surgery, ideally with time in a major trauma centre.
- Leading cardiac arrest and peri-arrest teams on call.
- Inter-hospital transfers, including time-critical ones.
- Burns cases if your region has a burns unit.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on major haemorrhage and head injury.
- Leading a resuscitation team, with a debrief, recorded as an A-CEX or reflection.
- Trauma and transfer courses and simulation.
