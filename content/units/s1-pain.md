## What the block is like

Pain experience comes from acute pain ward rounds with the inpatient pain team, your own postoperative patients, and at least an introduction to chronic pain (clinic or pain management programme).

## What you need to show

{{caps stage-1 pain}}

## Cases to seek out

- Acute pain rounds: PCA, epidural and nerve catheter reviews.
- Opioid-tolerant patients, and patients with renal or hepatic impairment.
- A pain clinic session to see the biopsychosocial model in practice.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on analgesic plans for complex patients.
- DOPS on setting up a PCA or epidural pump.
- Reflection on a pain clinic visit.
