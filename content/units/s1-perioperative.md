## What the block is like

Perioperative medicine runs through every list, but pre-operative assessment clinics and hip fracture lists are where you build it deliberately. Older patients with frailty and cognitive impairment are now the core of emergency surgery.

## Cases to seek out

- Pre-assessment clinic sessions, presenting your plans to a consultant.
- Hip fracture lists, including the emergency department block and delirium prevention.
- Day-case lists: selection, discharge criteria and advice.
- A patient who needs critical care after surgery: make the referral with a senior.

## Getting the evidence

- A-CEX or CBD from pre-assessment clinic (these also support EPA 1).
- Blood transfusion training and a CBD on blood product prescribing.
- A reflection on the environmental impact of anaesthesia, such as volatile choice and waste.
