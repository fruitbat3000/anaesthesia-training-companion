## What the block is like

The ICU block introduces you to organ support, ward referrals and the outreach team, daily ward rounds, and difficult conversations with families. You will insert central and arterial lines, assess deteriorating ward patients, and help run resuscitation and transfers. Supervision is measured in FICM capability levels (1 = direct supervision, 4 = independent), not the anaesthetic levels.

## Cases and skills to seek out

- Central venous, arterial and dialysis catheters (ultrasound-guided), recorded as DOPS.
- Chest drain and needle decompression in simulation.
- Admission decisions: take referrals with a senior and discuss why patients were or were not admitted.
- Intra-hospital transfers to CT and theatre.
- A family meeting about escalation or end-of-life care (observe first, then lead with support).

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on sepsis, organ failure and ICU discharge planning.
- Reflection on an end-of-life conversation (no patient-identifiable details).
- Adult life support and transfer courses.
