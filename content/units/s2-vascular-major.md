## Cases to seek out

- Open and endovascular aortic repair, and a ruptured aneurysm if you can.
- Carotid endarterectomy under general or regional anaesthesia.
- Major colorectal, upper GI, HPB, urological and gynaecological cancer surgery.
- Emergency laparotomy with NELA-style risk assessment.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on haemorrhage, viscoelastic testing and blood product use.
- ALMAT on running a major list.
- A-CEX on shared decision-making with a high-risk patient.
