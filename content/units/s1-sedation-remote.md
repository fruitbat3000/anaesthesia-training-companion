## Where to get it

- Sedation lists in theatre (for example for procedures under regional anaesthesia) and endoscopy where anaesthetists provide sedation.
- Anaesthesia in radiology, CT, MRI, ECT and the emergency department with a senior present.
- Trauma calls in the emergency department, and trauma simulation.

## Getting the evidence

- A-CEX on sedation, including assessment, monitoring and recovery.
- CBD on the risks of anaesthesia in a remote site and how you reduced them.
- Trauma simulation or a trauma course as a personal activity.

> [!key] Remote sites
> Check the equipment, the help and the route back to theatre before you start. The Association of Anaesthetists' monitoring standards apply wherever you anaesthetise.
