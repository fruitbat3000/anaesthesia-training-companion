> [!key] The Triple C form
> This is one of the four discrete areas of practice (cardiothoracic, neuro, obstetric and paediatric anaesthesia). A designated member of your Assessment Faculty with experience in the area can sign off its key capabilities on a **Completion of Capability Cluster ("Triple C") form**. You need the capabilities, suitable experience and logbook data, and a Multiple Trainer Report. If your ES can assess the evidence directly, a Triple C form is not strictly required.

## What the block is like

You will increasingly be the senior anaesthetist on labour ward out of hours: planning care with the multidisciplinary team, attending high-risk antenatal clinics, and leading the anaesthetic response to haemorrhage, eclampsia and collapse.

## Cases to seek out

- Obstetric anaesthesia antenatal clinic: complex cardiac, spinal or airway problems.
- Major obstetric haemorrhage and general anaesthesia for caesarean section.
- Pre-eclampsia and HELLP, including fluid and blood pressure management.
- Multidisciplinary obstetric emergency drills.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on complex antenatal plans, and an ALMAT on running labour ward.
- Obstetric emergency simulation.
- Postnatal follow-up of complications.
