## What the block is like

Stage 1 paediatric experience is usually gained in a district general hospital on elective and emergency lists for older children: ENT, dental, general surgery, orthopaedics and urology. The anaesthetic itself is often straightforward. The skills are in preparation, calculating doses, managing anxiety (the child's and the parents'), gas induction, and airway management in a smaller airway.

The supervision levels matter here: children aged 5–10 at level 2a (a supervisor in the theatre suite), and over 10 at level 2b (a supervisor in the hospital).

## Cases to seek out

- Tonsillectomy and other shared-airway ENT cases.
- Gas and intravenous inductions at different ages.
- Emergency appendicectomy with a rapid sequence induction.
- Paediatric recovery, pain and nausea management.

## Getting the evidence

- A-CEX or CBD on pre-operative assessment and consent with a parent.
- DOPS on cannulation and airway management.
- Paediatric life support course, and paediatric resuscitation simulation, for capability I.

> [!tip] Weight-based everything
> Write down the weight, fluid, drug and emergency doses before you start. Many departments have a paediatric drug chart or calculator. Use it every time.
