> [!key] The Triple C form
> This is one of the four discrete areas of practice (cardiothoracic, neuro, obstetric and paediatric anaesthesia). A designated member of your Assessment Faculty with experience in the area can sign off its key capabilities on a **Completion of Capability Cluster ("Triple C") form**. You need the capabilities, suitable experience and logbook data, and a Multiple Trainer Report. If your ES can assess the evidence directly, a Triple C form is not strictly required.

## What the block is like

Usually a placement in a regional cardiothoracic centre. Days start early with complex preparation: arterial and central lines, a pulmonary artery catheter or TOE in some cases, and bypass. Thoracic lists add lung isolation and one-lung ventilation. You will also see the cardiac ICU, where post-bypass physiology plays out.

## What you need to show

{{caps stage-2 ga O,P,Q,R}}

## Cases to seek out

- CABG and aortic valve replacement from induction to ICU handover.
- Off-pump surgery if your centre does it.
- Cath lab procedures: TAVI, ablation, device insertion, cardioversion.
- Lobectomy by VATS, with a double-lumen tube and bronchial blocker, and paravertebral or erector spinae analgesia.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on bypass physiology, weaning and post-bypass bleeding.
- DOPS on double-lumen tube placement and lung isolation checks.
- Keep your logbook tagged for the Triple C.
