> [!key] The Triple C form
> This is one of the four discrete areas of practice (cardiothoracic, neuro, obstetric and paediatric anaesthesia). A designated member of your Assessment Faculty with experience in the area can sign off its key capabilities on a **Completion of Capability Cluster ("Triple C") form**. You need the capabilities, suitable experience and logbook data, and a Multiple Trainer Report. If your ES can assess the evidence directly, a Triple C form is not strictly required.

## What the block is like

A placement in a regional neurosciences centre: elective craniotomy, spinal surgery, emergency evacuation of haematomas, interventional neuroradiology (coiling and thrombectomy), and neurocritical care. Neuroprotection, smooth emergence and early neurological assessment shape every anaesthetic.

## Cases to seek out

- Elective supratentorial craniotomy and spinal surgery (including prone positioning).
- Emergency craniotomy for traumatic or spontaneous haemorrhage.
- Coiling or thrombectomy in interventional radiology.
- Transfer of a patient with a head injury or acute neurological deterioration.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on raised intracranial pressure, subarachnoid haemorrhage and positioning.
- A-CEX in neuroradiology, a remote site with its own challenges.
- Transfer course or simulation as a personal activity.
