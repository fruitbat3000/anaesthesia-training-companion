## Where to get it

- An ICU block with on-call responsibility for referrals and admissions.
- Ventilation strategy in ARDS, renal replacement therapy, and sepsis bundles.
- Brainstem death testing and donor management with the specialist nurse for organ donation.

## Getting the evidence

- CBDs on ventilation, AKI and sepsis.
- DOPS on invasive procedures.
- Reflection on end-of-life care and family meetings.
