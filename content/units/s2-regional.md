## Where to get it

- Upper limb trauma and hand surgery lists for brachial plexus blocks.
- Lower limb arthroplasty and trauma lists.
- Rib fractures, laparotomy and breast surgery for fascial plane blocks.
- Regional anaesthesia in anticoagulated patients: follow local and national guidance.

## Getting the evidence

- DOPS for each block group, repeated as supervision distances.
- Part-task trainers and ultrasound courses.
- Follow-up of block complications on the ward.
