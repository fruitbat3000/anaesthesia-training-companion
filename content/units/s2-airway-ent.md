## What the block is like

ENT, head and neck and maxillofacial lists give the richest airway experience: shared airways, laser surgery, tracheostomy, and awake techniques for patients with airway pathology. Curriculum v1.5 (August 2026) expects most core airway skills to be independent (level 4) by the end of Stage 2.

## Cases to seek out

- Microlaryngoscopy, laser surgery and jet ventilation.
- Flexible bronchoscopic intubation, including awake, and intubation through a supraglottic airway.
- Tracheostomy and laryngectomy patients.
- Emergency airway infections (for example quinsy, epiglottitis, dental abscess).

## Getting the evidence

- DOPS for each airway technique (the v1.5 capabilities are specific).
- CBDs on airway planning, including extubation strategy.
- Front-of-neck access and "can't intubate, can't oxygenate" simulation.
