stem: Which nerve provides sensory innervation to the laryngeal mucosa above the vocal cords?
A: External branch of the superior laryngeal nerve
B: Glossopharyngeal nerve
C: Hypoglossal nerve
D: Internal branch of the superior laryngeal nerve
E: Recurrent laryngeal nerve
answer: D
