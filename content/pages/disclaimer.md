## Doses, thresholds and guidelines

Where doses, drug concentrations or physiological thresholds are given, they are there to illustrate principles for examination revision. **Always check doses against the current British National Formulary (BNF), the product's Summary of Product Characteristics, and your local policies before prescribing or administering any drug.** Guidelines change. Follow the current version of any guideline and your hospital's own protocols.

## Review status

Most content on this site is marked **Draft**: it has been written and referenced but has not yet been checked by a clinical reviewer. Content marked **Reviewed** has been checked by a named reviewer, but may still contain errors. Please report anything that looks wrong using the "Report a problem" link on the page.

## Your data

The site does not collect personal data. Progress (ticks, answers, station records) is stored only in your own browser and is never sent anywhere. Never enter patient-identifiable information into any part of the site, including station comments.
