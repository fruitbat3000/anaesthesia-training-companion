## Support

| Resource | What it offers |
|---|---|
| [RCoA wellbeing and support (Novice Guide)](https://www.rcoa.ac.uk/documents/novice-guide/wellbeing-support) | The College's signposting of support for anaesthetists in training |
| [Practitioner Health](https://www.practitionerhealth.nhs.uk/) | Free, confidential NHS service for doctors with mental health or addiction concerns |
| [BMA wellbeing support](https://www.bma.org.uk/advice-and-support/your-wellbeing) | 24/7 counselling and peer support (open to all doctors) |
| [Samaritans](https://www.samaritans.org/) | 24-hour listening service: call 116 123 |
| Your Educational Supervisor, College Tutor and TPD | First point of contact for training concerns |
| [Yorkshire and Humber sources of help](https://www.yorksandhumberdeanery.nhs.uk/anaesthesia/school-information/sources-help) | The school's own list of support |

## Flexibility in training

The RCoA's [flexibility in training](https://www.rcoa.ac.uk/training-careers/training-hub/flexibility-training) pages cover **less than full-time training (LTFT)**, **out of programme** opportunities (OOPT, OOPR, OOPE, OOPC, OOPP), and **returning to training** after time away. Returners should also use the NHS England SuppoRTT programme and talk to their TPD before returning.

## Fatigue

Night shifts and long days affect performance and safety. Plan your journey home after nights, use rest facilities, and speak up if a rota is unsafe. The Association of Anaesthetists and the RCoA publish fatigue resources for anaesthetists; ask your department where to find local rest facilities.
