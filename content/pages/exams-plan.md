## The principle

The new exams reward **understanding you can apply**. The most efficient revision alternates three activities, each feeding the others:

:::cols
::: card 1. Learn
Read a [revision note](#notes) and the linked e-LA session or reference. Aim to explain the topic out loud in two minutes.
:::
::: card 2. Test
Answer the linked [questions](#questions). Read the explanation for every option, including the ones you got right.
:::
::: card 3. Apply
Use it at work the same week: talk it through with your supervisor, or run a [station](#stations) on it with a colleague.
:::
:::

## Primary: a 16-week outline

| Weeks | Focus | Questions |
|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Orientation. Read the [syllabus coverage map](#notes/coverage/primary) and the RCoA sample questions. Take a 20-question diagnostic from each subject | 100 |
| 3–6 | **Physiology** (respiratory, cardiovascular, renal, neuro), then **pharmacology** principles (PK/PD, receptors) | 40–60 a week |
| 7–10 | **Pharmacology** by drug group; **physics** (gases, flow, vapours, electricity, ultrasound) | 60–80 a week |
| 11–12 | **Clinical measurement, data interpretation, anatomy and statistics** | 80 a week |
| 13–14 | Mock Paper A and Paper B under timed conditions; revise weak areas from the results page | Mocks + retries |
| 15–16 | Final mocks; "questions I got wrong" and flagged questions; rest before the exam | Mocks |

Start CASE preparation once the AKT is passed: two or three [CASE stations](#stations) a week with a partner, plus deliberate practice at work (explaining your anaesthetic plan, interpreting data aloud, equipment checks).

## Final: a 20-week outline

| Weeks | Focus |
|---|---|
| 1–2 | Diagnostic questions from each domain; list the areas you have not yet seen clinically |
| 3–10 | **General anaesthesia by specialty** (half the paper): airway, obstetrics, paediatrics, neuro, cardiac and thoracic, vascular, and the other specialties |
| 11–13 | **Perioperative medicine** and **regional anaesthesia** |
| 14–16 | **Intensive care, pain, trauma and transfer, professional domains** |
| 17–20 | Timed Final AKT mocks; targeted review; start FCPE station practice with a partner |

## Habits that help

- **Little and often.** Twenty questions a day beats two hundred on a Sunday.
- **Review your mistakes.** Use "Retry the ones I got wrong" and the flag button. The [progress page](#questions/stats) shows weak areas.
- **Talk it through.** Explaining out loud is the single best preparation for CASE and FCPE, and it is how you will be marked.
- **Use your portfolio.** CBDs and A-CEXs on exam topics count twice: evidence for your HALOs and rehearsal for the clinical exam.
- **Look after yourself.** See [wellbeing and flexibility](#resources/wellbeing).
