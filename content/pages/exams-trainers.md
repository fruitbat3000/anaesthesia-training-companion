## What has changed in the assessment philosophy

The RCoA describes a shift from recall-heavy, examiner-led questioning with separate science and clinical sections, to **reasoning-focused, candidate-led, integrated assessment in authentic clinical encounters**. The standard and syllabus are unchanged. Knowledge still matters, but candidates must show how it informs safe decisions.

Both clinical exams (CASE and FCPE) are marked on **key features** (what the candidate identifies and does) and **domains** (communication, clinical reasoning, organisation, situational awareness, task management, professionalism), with a **global rating** anchored to workplace supervision:

| Global rating | Workplace equivalent |
|---|---|
| Clear fail | Always needs direct help and guidance; could not be left unsupervised |
| Borderline fail | Some ability but needs frequent help; supervisor must be close by |
| Borderline pass | Does the basics unaided; needs guidance with complex tasks; supervisor must be able to attend |
| Good pass | Independent for most things; supervisor by phone |
| Excellent pass | Capable and independent |

This mirrors the entrustment language trainers already use for HALOs and EPAs, so **your usual workplace judgements translate directly**.

## Practical ideas for departments

:::cols
::: card In theatre
- Ask the trainee to **think aloud**: their plan, the science behind it, and what they will do if it goes wrong.
- Pause at key moments ("Why that dose? What is the MAC doing now?").
- Let them lead the team brief and the handover to recovery.
:::
::: card In teaching
- Run **nine-minute structured discussions** using this site's [station packs](#stations); one trainee is the candidate, one the examiner.
- Integrate the science into clinical cases rather than teaching it separately.
- Build in data interpretation: gases, ECGs, charts, imaging, PFTs.
:::
::: card In assessment
- Use CBDs and A-CEXs on exam-relevant cases and explore "what if" branches.
- Rehearse emergencies in simulation, including one-to-one manikin scenarios.
- Give feedback in the key-features-and-domains language above.
:::
:::
