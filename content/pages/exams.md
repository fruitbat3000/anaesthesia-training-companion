> [!exam] The short version
> - **From July 2027** the Primary becomes an **Applied Knowledge Test (AKT)** of 2 × 80 single-best-answer questions plus **CASE**, a 13-station clinical exam. The Final becomes a 100-question **AKT** plus **FCPE**, a 12-station clinical exam.
> - The CRQ paper, the OSCE and both SOEs are retired. There is no "rest station" and no killer station.
> - **July 2026 – June 2027** is the last year of the legacy formats. **July 2027 – June 2028** is a transition year in which legacy OSCE/SOE sittings are open only to candidates holding a partial pass.
> - Attempts restart at 1 on the new format; the maximum of six attempts per component still applies.

{{exam-table}}

## Why it is changing

The redesign follows the RCoA's internal review and the Independent Review of RCoA examination processes (both 2023). The College's stated aims are to assess **reasoning rather than recall**, to integrate the basic sciences with clinical work, to make stations more like real clinical encounters, and to stop re-testing Primary knowledge in the Final. Direct knowledge testing moves mainly into the written AKTs; the clinical exams test how you apply it.

:::cols
::: card What is examined
The **same syllabus**. The Primary tests Stage 1 of the 2021 curriculum; the Final tests Stage 2. The syllabus codes used on this site's notes come from the RCoA's Primary syllabus (v2.2, January 2026) and Stage 2 examination syllabus.
:::
::: card How the clinical exams are marked
Each station has **key features** (what you identify and act on) and **domains** (communication, clinical reasoning, situational awareness, task management, professionalism). Examiners also give a **global rating** used only to set the pass mark by borderline regression.
:::
::: card What to practise
Thinking aloud, prioritising, explaining your plan to a colleague or patient, interpreting data (ECGs, gases, charts, imaging) and managing an evolving emergency. See the [station practice packs](#stations).
:::
:::
