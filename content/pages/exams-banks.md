> [!tip] Format note
> Nearly all existing banks were written for the legacy exams (the 90-question Primary MCQ and the Final CRQ/MCQ). The single-best-answer style carries over to the new AKTs. At the time of writing, no bank targets CASE or FCPE stations; use this site's [station packs](#stations) and practice with colleagues.

## Books

Several SBA and revision books are published for both parts of the exam. Check that any book you buy is recent: older books include question types (multiple true/false, CRQ, SOE) that will not appear in the new format. Your department library or the RCoA library may hold copies.
