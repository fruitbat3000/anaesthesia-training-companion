## Primary AKT (Applied Knowledge Test)

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | Two papers of **80 single-best-answer (SBA)** questions, **2 h 20 min** each, sat on **separate days** |
| Delivery | Online with remote invigilation (TestReach); **twice a year, September and March** |
| Paper A | **Pharmacology 40**, **physiology 40** |
| Paper B | **Physics 30**, **data interpretation & clinical measurement 35**, **anatomy 10**, **statistics 5** |
| Result | The two papers are **one component**: performance is combined and there is no separate pass for each paper |
| Standard setting | Modified Angoff (as for the current MCQ) |

Each question has a clinical stem, a lead-in and five options, one of which is best. The RCoA emphasises **application**: questions set the science in a clinical context and ask how it informs a decision. Build stamina for two long papers.

**Before July 2027 (legacy):** the Primary MCQ is a single 3-hour paper of 90 SBA questions: 30 pharmacology, 30 physiology (with biochemistry and anatomy), and 30 physics, clinical measurement, statistics and data interpretation.

## Primary CASE (Clinical Anaesthetic Sciences Examination)

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | **13 stations of 9 minutes**, with 2 minutes between stations to reset and read the next brief (2 h 23 min in total) |
| Delivery | In person; three diets a year (**October, February, June**), each over 4–5 days; you attend only for your time slot |
| Replaces | The OSCE and the SOE |
| Assesses | Clinical application of the basic sciences, clinical reasoning, critical thinking, communication, risk assessment, clinical management and professional behaviours |

Stations may use role players, simulation (one station uses a high-fidelity manikin), anaesthetic equipment, anaesthetic and observation charts, drug charts and notes, and data such as blood results, gases, ECGs, radiographs, CT, pulmonary function tests and monitoring. Some are "professional conversations" with an examiner, like planning a case with your supervisor. You may still be asked to draw a graph or write an equation.

**Every circuit includes at least** (a station can count towards more than one):

:::cols
::: card Clinical arena
Preoperative 4 · Intraoperative 5 · Postoperative 1 · Critically ill patient 2 · Practice outside theatre 1
:::
::: card Basic science focus
Physiology 2 · Pharmacology 2 · Physics & equipment 3 · Anatomy 1
:::
::: card Patients and domains
Adult, paediatric, obstetric and elderly patients (1 each). Perioperative medicine, GA, regional, resuscitation & transfer, pain and ICM (1 each). Three stations each for the three Good Medical Practice domains.
:::
:::

## Eligibility and practicalities

- Validity: a Primary pass is valid for 7 years; a written (AKT/MCQ) pass for 3 years. The RCoA will extend written validity by one year on request for candidates waiting for the new clinical exam.
- Maximum six attempts at each component.
- Fees for the new format had not been published as of September 2026.
- Results for the clinical exam: typically 4–5 weeks, with feedback by domain and an examiner comment per station (Achieved / Borderline / Did not achieve).

## Preparing

- **AKT:** work through the [Primary revision notes](#notes/primary) against the [syllabus coverage map](#notes/coverage/primary), and practise with the [question bank](#questions) and [mock papers](#questions/mock).
- **CASE:** use the [CASE station packs](#stations) with a colleague or trainer. Practise explaining the science behind what you do in theatre: why this drug, why this monitor, why this position.
- Use your workplace: pre-assessment clinic, theatre briefings, recovery handovers, ICU and emergency simulation are all rehearsal for CASE.
- Regional exam teaching in Yorkshire and Humber is run through [YAIRN](https://yairn.co.uk/).
