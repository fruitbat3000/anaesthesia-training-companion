## Final AKT

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | **One paper of 100 SBA questions, 3 hours**. There is **no CRQ paper** |
| Content | **General anaesthesia 50**, **perioperative medicine 10**, **regional anaesthesia 10**, **other curriculum domains 30** (pain, intensive care, resuscitation and transfer, sedation, professional domains) |
| First sitting | **January 2028**; then twice a year |
| Emphasis | Higher-order clinical reasoning, interpretation of complexity and management decisions |

**Before 2028 (legacy):** the Final written exam is a 3-hour Constructed Response Question (CRQ) paper of 12 questions plus a separate MCQ paper, sat on different days. A pass in the legacy written exam remains valid for 3 years and carries forward to the FCPE.

## FCPE (Final Clinical Performance Examination)

| | |
|---|---|
| Format | **12 stations of 9 minutes** with 2-minute transitions (2 h 12 min) |
| Delivery | In person; **two diets a year (November and April)**, each over 5 days |
| Replaces | The Final SOE |
| Assesses | Managing complexity and uncertainty, advanced clinical reasoning, communication, appropriate leadership and safe performance in realistic Stage 2 scenarios |

**Every circuit includes at least:** preoperative 2, intraoperative 2, postoperative 2, critically ill patient 1 and practice outside theatre 2. Each clinical domain (perioperative medicine, GA, regional, resuscitation & transfer, pain, ICM) appears at least once. Over an exam week there are **at least two neuro, two cardiac and two airway stations**, and a bariatric station. Paediatric, obstetric, elderly, general and complex-comorbidity patients appear in every circuit. There are three stations each for the three Good Medical Practice domains.

The RCoA's advice is to prepare across the whole Stage 2 curriculum rather than expecting particular stations. Candidates should talk to their Educational Supervisor or College Tutor about timing, for example whether to wait until they have had cardiac or neuro experience.

## Eligibility

- You must have passed the Primary (or hold an exempting qualification). The RCoA recommends holding the Stage 1 certificate and **strongly recommends not attempting the clinical exam until you have started Stage 2**.
- Candidates who pass the current Primary by June 2027 sit the new Final.
- Maximum six attempts at each component; attempts restart at 1 on the new format.

## Preparing

- Work through the [Final revision notes](#notes/final), weighted towards general anaesthesia, and the [Final question bank](#questions).
- For the FCPE, practise structured thinking out loud: gather information, prioritise, plan, explain your reasoning, anticipate complications and stay situationally aware. The [FCPE station packs](#stations) follow this structure.
- CBD-style discussions, theatre debriefs, ICU referrals, transfer planning and complex perioperative planning conversations with consultants are the best rehearsal.
