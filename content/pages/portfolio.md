## The building blocks

:::cols
::: card HALOs
**Holistic Assessment of Learning Outcomes**: one per domain per stage. Your supervisor signs a HALO when your evidence as a whole shows you meet the stage's learning outcome. There is no fixed number of assessments.
:::
::: card SLEs
**Supervised Learning Events**: A-CEX, ALMAT, A-QIPAT, CBD and DOPS. Low-stakes, formative, with a supervision level. Progress is shown by the supervision level rising over time.
:::
::: card Other evidence
Personal activities (courses, teaching, e-learning, simulation), personal reflections, logbook, MSF, MTRs and the ESSR.
:::
:::

## What every ARCP needs

- **ESSR** (Educational Supervisor's Structured Report), submitted at least two weeks before the ARCP, with correct dates and placements.
- **Exams** entered with dates and the pass letters uploaded. Primary by end of CT3; Final by end of ST5.
- **Personal development plan**: objectives for the year (can sit within supervisor meeting records).
- **Logbook**: preferably the LLP logbook; otherwise the RCoA summary dataset for the period and cumulatively from the start of Stage 1.
- **At least three supervisor meetings** a year.
- **SLEs** showing progression.
- **MSF**: at least one per ARCP, with at least 12 responses.
- **MTR**: at least one a year, plus MTRs for the IAC and IACOA, and to support Triple C forms.
- **Quality improvement**: involvement in audit or QI in each stage (national projects such as NAPs count), increasing with seniority; the A-QIPAT documents it. Annual evidence of clinical governance activity.
- **Form R** (revalidation) covering your whole scope of practice, including any serious incidents, which you should reflect on with your ES.
- **Reflection**: no set number, but you must show your ES that you are a reflective practitioner who learns from excellence as well as error.

## HALOs by year

| Year | HALO requirement |
|---|---|
| CT1 | EPA 1 and EPA 2 (the IAC) |
| CT2 | EPA 3 and EPA 4 |
| CT3 | All Stage 1 HALOs (v1.4 or v1.5), including evidence of airway practical procedures / new airway key capabilities and the paediatric resuscitation key capability |
| ST4 | Progress with Stage 2 |
| ST5 | All Stage 2 HALOs; Triple C forms for cardiothoracic, neuro, obstetric and paediatric areas |
| ST6 | Progress with Stage 3; SIA evidence if started |
| ST7 | All Stage 3 HALOs and HALOs for your SIAs |

> [!warn] Before deleting a HALO
> After August 2026 all new HALOs are v1.5. Deleting a v1.4 HALO that is in progress cannot be undone. At the end of a stage, delete and recreate any in-progress HALOs for the next stage before you start it.

## Good habits

- Link every piece of evidence to the relevant key capabilities as you go, not the week before the ARCP.
- Ask for SLEs on the day, while the case is fresh, and choose cases that stretch you.
- Reflect on things that went well, not just critical incidents.
- Never include patient-identifiable information.
- This site's [station practice](#stations) generates a feedback summary you can use to support a reflection or SLE discussion.
