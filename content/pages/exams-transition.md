## Key dates

| Period | What runs |
|---|---|
| July 2026 – June 2027 | **Final year of the legacy formats**: Primary MCQ, OSCE and SOE; Final written (CRQ + MCQ) and SOE |
| July 2027 – June 2028 | **Transition year**. All first-time candidates sit the new formats. Legacy Primary OSCE/SOE sittings continue **only** for candidates with a valid partial pass |
| January 2028 | First Final AKT |
| From July 2028 | New formats only. Partial passes in legacy components no longer carry forward |

## Common situations

:::steps
1. **I have not sat the Primary yet.** From July 2027 you sit the Primary AKT and then CASE. Sittings before then are legacy format.
2. **I passed the Primary MCQ but have not sat the OSCE/SOE.** From July 2027 you sit CASE. Your written pass remains valid for 3 years, and the RCoA will extend it by a year on request if you want to wait for CASE.
3. **I passed one of the OSCE or SOE but not the other.** During 2027–28 you may either complete the outstanding legacy component (it must be passed by the final transition sitting in June 2028) or give up the partial pass and sit CASE (at a reduced fee in 2027–28).
4. **I will pass the Primary before July 2027.** You will sit the new Final (AKT and FCPE).
5. **I passed the legacy Final written exam.** It remains valid for 3 years and allows you to sit the FCPE.
:::

## Attempts and validity

- Your first attempt at a new component counts as **attempt 1**, even if you have had several attempts at the legacy equivalent. The maximum of six attempts per component is unchanged.
- If you have **used all six attempts** at a legacy component, you cannot sit its new equivalent.
- A Primary pass is valid for **7 years**; written passes (MCQ, CRQ, AKT) for **3 years**.
- If you cannot sit during the transition year (for example because of health or parental leave), you move to the new format on your return. The June 2028 cut-off will not be extended.

## Official decision trees

The RCoA publishes decision trees for the Primary OSCE/SOE and the Final SOE on its [new exam format FAQs page](https://www.rcoa.ac.uk/examinations/2027-launch-new-frca-exams/new-exam-format-faqs) and a planning page with exam pathways. Email exams@rcoa.ac.uk with individual questions.
