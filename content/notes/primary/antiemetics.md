## Classes

| Class | Examples | Site / receptor | Side effects |
|---|---|---|---|
| **5-HT~3~ antagonists** | Ondansetron, granisetron, palonosetron | CTZ and vagal afferents | Headache, constipation, **QT prolongation** |
| **Corticosteroids** | **Dexamethasone** (e.g. 4–8 mg IV at induction) | Uncertain (central) | Hyperglycaemia; perineal burning with rapid IV injection; debated wound infection risk |
| **D~2~ antagonists** | Droperidol, haloperidol; metoclopramide (also prokinetic); domperidone | CTZ | **Extrapyramidal effects** (dystonia, especially in young women; treat with procyclidine), sedation, QT prolongation, neuroleptic malignant syndrome (rare); avoid metoclopramide in bowel obstruction and Parkinson's disease |
| **Antihistamines (H~1~)** | Cyclizine, promethazine | Vomiting centre, vestibular | Sedation, dry mouth, **tachycardia**, injection pain |
| **Antimuscarinics** | Hyoscine (transdermal patch) | Vestibular, vomiting centre | Dry mouth, blurred vision, confusion in the elderly |
| **NK~1~ antagonists** | Aprepitant, fosaprepitant | Substance P receptors in the nucleus tractus solitarius and CTZ | Long-acting; interactions (CYP3A4, oral contraceptives) |
| **Others** | Propofol (TIVA), dexmedetomidine, ondansetron + dexamethasone combinations | | |

## Risk and prophylaxis (Fourth consensus guidelines)

- **Apfel score (adults):** female, non-smoker, history of PONV or motion sickness, postoperative opioids. Each adds risk (roughly 10%, 20%, 40%, 60%, 80% for 0–4 factors).
- **Reduce baseline risk:** regional anaesthesia where appropriate, **propofol TIVA**, avoid N~2~O and volatile agents in high-risk patients, **opioid-sparing** multimodal analgesia, adequate hydration.
- **Multimodal prophylaxis:** give **two agents** from different classes to patients with one or two risk factors, and three or more when risk is high; many centres now give two agents to most patients having general anaesthesia.
- **Rescue:** use an antiemetic from a **different class** from those already given (repeating the same drug within about 6 hours is ineffective).
- Children: POVOC score (surgery ≥ 30 min, age ≥ 3 years, strabismus surgery, history of PONV).
