## Bronchodilators and anti-inflammatories

| Class | Examples | Mechanism | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **β~2~-agonists** | Salbutamol (short), salmeterol and formoterol (long) | G~s~ → ↑ cAMP → smooth muscle relaxation | Tremor, tachycardia, **hypokalaemia**, lactic acidosis |
| **Antimuscarinics** | Ipratropium (short), tiotropium (long) | M~3~ blockade | Especially useful in COPD |
| **Methylxanthines** | Aminophylline, theophylline | PDE inhibition, adenosine antagonism | **Narrow therapeutic index**; arrhythmias, seizures; CYP1A2 interactions (smoking lowers, erythromycin/ciprofloxacin raise levels) |
| **Corticosteroids** | Inhaled (beclometasone, budesonide); systemic (prednisolone, hydrocortisone) | Anti-inflammatory; ↑ β-receptor expression | Take hours to act |
| **Leukotriene antagonists** | Montelukast | Block CysLT~1~ | Aspirin-sensitive asthma |
| **Magnesium sulphate** | | Ca^2+^ antagonism in smooth muscle | Acute severe asthma (single IV dose) |
| **Volatile anaesthetics** | Sevoflurane, isoflurane | Direct relaxation, reduced vagal tone | Refractory bronchospasm |
| **Ketamine** | | Sympathomimetic, direct relaxation | Induction in severe asthma |

## Bronchospasm under anaesthesia

Stop the stimulus (light anaesthesia, airway instrumentation, a drug), 100% O~2~, deepen anaesthesia with volatile, **salbutamol** (inhaled into the circuit or IV), consider adrenaline, magnesium, aminophylline and ketamine; exclude mimics (tube problems, endobronchial intubation, anaphylaxis, pneumothorax, aspiration). Allow a long expiratory time to avoid gas trapping (dynamic hyperinflation → hypotension). The QRH has a bronchospasm card.

## Oxygen as a drug

- Prescribe to a **target saturation**: **94–98%** for most acutely ill patients; **88–92%** for those at risk of hypercapnic respiratory failure (BTS guideline).
- **Toxicity:** absorption atelectasis, pulmonary toxicity with prolonged high FiO~2~, CNS toxicity under hyperbaric conditions, retinopathy of prematurity, hypercapnia in susceptible patients, and fire risk.
- **Devices:** variable performance (nasal cannulae, simple face masks, non-rebreathe masks) versus fixed performance (**Venturi masks**; high-flow nasal oxygen).

## Other drugs

- **Respiratory stimulants:** doxapram (peripheral chemoreceptors).
- **Pulmonary vasodilators:** inhaled nitric oxide, prostacyclin (epoprostenol, iloprost), sildenafil (PDE5), bosentan (endothelin antagonist).
- **Mucolytics:** acetylcysteine, dornase alfa (cystic fibrosis). **Surfactant** for neonatal RDS.
