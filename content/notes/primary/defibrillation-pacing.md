## Defibrillators

- A **capacitor** is charged from a DC source (via a transformer and rectifier) and discharged through the patient; an **inductor** shapes and prolongs the discharge. Stored energy = **½CV^2^**; not all stored energy is delivered.
- Defibrillation depolarises a **critical mass of myocardium** simultaneously, allowing the SA node to resume control.
- **Biphasic** waveforms (current reverses direction) achieve defibrillation at **lower energies** than monophasic and are now standard (typically 120–200 J initially, per manufacturer; RCUK recommends at least 150 J for the first shock with biphasic devices, then fixed or escalating energies).
- **Thoracic impedance** (typically 50–150 Ω) reduces current: reduced by correct pad size and position (right infraclavicular and left mid-axillary apex, or anteroposterior), good contact (gel pads), shocking at end-expiration, and firm paddle pressure. Modern devices measure and compensate for impedance.
- **Synchronised cardioversion** delivers the shock on the **R wave** to avoid the vulnerable period on the T wave (which could induce VF): used for tachyarrhythmias with a pulse.
- **Safety:** remove oxygen sources during shock (fire), no one touching the patient or bed, "stand clear" call; minimise pauses in compressions (charge during compressions). **AEDs** analyse rhythm and advise shocks.

## Pacemakers

- **Generic code (NBG):** position I **chamber paced** (O, A, V, D); II **chamber sensed** (O, A, V, D); III **response to sensing** (O none, T triggered, I inhibited, D dual); IV **rate modulation** (R); V multisite pacing.
- Example: **DDDR** — dual chamber pacing and sensing, dual response, rate adaptive. **VOO** — asynchronous ventricular pacing (as with a magnet on many pacemakers).
- **ICDs** detect and treat VT/VF (antitachycardia pacing, shocks). A **magnet** usually **suspends tachyarrhythmia therapies** in ICDs but **does not** change pacing mode (behaviour varies by device and must be confirmed).
- **Perioperative management:** know the device type, indication, pacing dependence and last check; involve the cardiac physiologist; deactivate ICD therapies for surgery with monopolar diathermy above the umbilicus (with external defibrillator pads on); consider reprogramming pacing-dependent patients to asynchronous mode; use bipolar diathermy or keep monopolar current away from the device; recheck the device afterwards. **MRI** needs specific protocols (MR-conditional devices).
- **Temporary pacing:** transcutaneous, transvenous or epicardial; threshold testing; microshock precautions.
