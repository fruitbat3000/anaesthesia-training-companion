## Insulins

| Type | Examples | Onset / duration (approx.) |
|---|---|---|
| Rapid-acting analogues | Aspart, lispro, glulisine | 10–20 min / 3–5 h |
| Short-acting (soluble) | Actrapid, Humulin S | 30 min / 6–8 h; used IV in VRIII |
| Intermediate (isophane) | Humulin I, Insulatard | 1–2 h / 12–18 h |
| Long-acting analogues | Glargine, detemir, degludec | Flat profile, ~24 h (degludec > 40 h) |
| Mixtures | Novomix 30, Humalog Mix | Biphasic |

**Type 1 diabetes: never stop basal insulin** — without it, ketoacidosis follows within hours. Continue long-acting basal insulin, usually at about **80%** of the dose, around surgery.

## Non-insulin drugs

| Drug | Mechanism | Perioperative risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Metformin** | ↓ hepatic gluconeogenesis, ↑ insulin sensitivity | Lactic acidosis (rare; renal impairment, contrast, hypoperfusion) |
| **Sulphonylureas** (gliclazide) | Close K~ATP~ channels → insulin release | **Hypoglycaemia** when fasting |
| **SGLT2 inhibitors** (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin) | Glycosuria | **Euglycaemic diabetic ketoacidosis** (ketones high, glucose may be near normal) — usually stopped for a defined period before surgery (per current guidance) |
| **GLP-1 receptor agonists** (semaglutide, liraglutide, tirzepatide as a GIP/GLP-1 agonist) | ↑ insulin, ↓ glucagon, **delayed gastric emptying** | **Residual gastric contents and aspiration risk** despite fasting; follow current multisociety guidance |
| **DPP-4 inhibitors** (sitagliptin) | ↑ incretins | Low hypoglycaemia risk |
| Pioglitazone | PPAR-γ agonist | Fluid retention, heart failure |

## Perioperative principles (Association of Anaesthetists 2015; updated multidisciplinary guidance 2025)

- **Optimise before elective surgery:** the 2015 guidance suggested referral for optimisation if HbA1c was above about **69 mmol/mol** (8.5%); check the current guideline and local pathway.
- **Short starvation**, first on the list; aim for **capillary glucose 6–10 mmol/L** (up to 12 acceptable).
- **Modify** medication using a **drug-by-drug** protocol; resume when eating.
- **Variable-rate IV insulin infusion (VRIII)** with a glucose-containing fluid (and K^+^) for long starvation (more than one missed meal), poorly controlled diabetes, or type 1 patients who cannot use their usual regimen; continue basal insulin.
- **Hypoglycaemia (< 4 mmol/L):** treat promptly (IV glucose if unconscious or fasting).
- Watch for **DKA** (type 1 and SGLT2 users) and **hyperosmolar hyperglycaemic state**.
