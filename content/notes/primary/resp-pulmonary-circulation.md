## Pressures and resistance

- A low-pressure, high-flow, highly compliant circuit: PA pressure about **25/10 mmHg (mean ~15)**, left atrial pressure ~5–8 mmHg.
- **PVR = (mean PAP − LAP) / CO × 80** dyn·s·cm^−5^; normal about **100–200 dyn·s·cm^−5^** (about one tenth of SVR).
- PVR **falls as cardiac output rises**, by **recruitment** of closed capillaries and **distension** of open ones, so pressure rises little with exercise.
- PVR is lowest at **FRC**: at low lung volumes extra-alveolar vessels narrow; at high lung volumes alveolar vessels are compressed.

## Hypoxic pulmonary vasoconstriction (HPV)

- Pulmonary arterioles constrict in response to **low alveolar PO~2~** (and to a lesser extent low mixed venous PO~2~), diverting blood to better-ventilated lung and reducing shunt.
- Mechanism: inhibition of oxygen-sensitive potassium channels in smooth muscle → depolarisation → calcium entry.
- Enhanced by acidosis; inhibited by alkalosis, vasodilators (nitroprusside, GTN), and (weakly, dose-dependently) by volatile anaesthetics. Largely preserved with propofol.
- Relevant in one-lung ventilation; global HPV (altitude, chronic hypoxia) causes pulmonary hypertension.

## Non-respiratory functions

- **Filter:** traps small emboli, thrombi and air bubbles.
- **Reservoir:** holds about 500 mL of blood, which can move into the systemic circulation.
- **Metabolic:** the endothelium converts **angiotensin I to angiotensin II** (ACE), inactivates **bradykinin**, and removes much of circulating **serotonin** and **noradrenaline** and some prostaglandins. Adrenaline, histamine, isoprenaline and angiotensin II pass through largely unchanged.
- **Drug handling:** first-pass uptake of basic lipophilic drugs such as fentanyl and lidocaine.
- **Defence:** mucociliary clearance, alveolar macrophages, IgA.
- Surfactant production; heat and water loss; acid–base regulation via CO~2~.
