## General approach

- **Recognise, call for help early, declare the problem**, and use the **Quick Reference Handbook (QRH)** with a reader. Use a structured approach (e.g. **ABCDE**, or a "COVER ABCD" check of the circuit, oxygen, ventilation, endotracheal tube, review of monitors and equipment, then airway, breathing, circulation, drugs).
- Allocate roles, share the mental model, verbalise plans, and re-evaluate.

## Unexpected desaturation

**100% O~2~** and hand-ventilate to feel compliance. Then work from machine to patient: oxygen supply and analyser, circuit leaks and disconnection, tube position (oesophageal, endobronchial, displaced, blocked, kinked), breathing (bronchospasm, pneumothorax, aspiration, atelectasis, pulmonary oedema), circulation (low cardiac output, PE), equipment (probe position, perfusion). Confirm with capnography.

## High airway pressure

**Equipment:** kinked or blocked tube or circuit, tube too far in, valve failure. **Patient:** bronchospasm, light anaesthesia and coughing, chest wall rigidity (opioids), pneumothorax, endobronchial intubation, aspiration, abdominal insufflation or steep Trendelenburg. Disconnect and hand-ventilate; pass a suction catheter down the tube.

## Hypotension

Rate (brady- or tachyarrhythmia), preload (hypovolaemia, bleeding, IPPV, aortocaval compression, pneumothorax, tamponade, embolism), afterload (anaesthetic vasodilation, neuraxial block, sepsis, anaphylaxis), contractility (ischaemia, drugs, acidosis). Check the measurement, reduce anaesthetic depth, fluids, vasopressors, and look for the cause.

## Other scenarios (see QRH cards)

- **Hypertension:** light anaesthesia and pain, hypercapnia, hypoxia, drug error, pre-eclampsia, phaeochromocytoma, raised ICP (Cushing's).
- **Arrhythmias:** correct oxygenation, ventilation and electrolytes; treat per RCUK peri-arrest algorithms.
- **Convulsions:** exclude hypoxia, hypoglycaemia and local anaesthetic toxicity; benzodiazepine; protect the airway.
- **Gas embolism** (e.g. laparoscopy, sitting craniotomy, central lines): sudden fall in ETCO~2~, hypotension, desaturation: flood the field, stop insufflation, 100% O~2~, left lateral head-down, aspirate via CVC, support circulation. **Fat embolism** after long-bone fractures; **bone cement implantation syndrome**.
- **Inadvertent intra-arterial injection:** leave the cannula in, flush with saline, analgesia, consider heparin and sympathetic block, seek vascular advice.
- **Accidental decannulation of a tracheostomy** or extubation in the prone position: follow the relevant algorithms.
- **Raised ICP / brainstem herniation:** head-up, hyperventilate briefly, osmotherapy, urgent neurosurgical input.

## Afterwards

Debrief the team; tell the patient what happened and apologise (**duty of candour**); document fully; report through the local incident system; discuss at morbidity and mortality meetings; arrange follow-up and support for staff.
