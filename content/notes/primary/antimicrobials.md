## Classes

| Class | Mechanism | Examples | Anaesthetic relevance |
|---|---|---|---|
| **β-lactams** | Cell wall synthesis (penicillin-binding proteins); bactericidal | Penicillins, co-amoxiclav, piperacillin–tazobactam, cephalosporins (cefuroxime, ceftriaxone), carbapenems (meropenem) | Allergy (true penicillin allergy much rarer than reported; cross-reactivity with cephalosporins low); **co-amoxiclav a common NAP6 trigger** |
| **Glycopeptides** | Cell wall (Gram-positive, including MRSA) | Vancomycin, **teicoplanin** | Vancomycin: **"red man" syndrome** with fast infusion (histamine); nephrotoxic. **Teicoplanin had the highest anaphylaxis rate in NAP6** |
| **Aminoglycosides** | 30S ribosome; bactericidal | Gentamicin | Nephro- and **ototoxic**; **potentiate neuromuscular block** |
| **Macrolides** | 50S ribosome | Erythromycin, clarithromycin | **CYP3A4 inhibition** (↑ midazolam, alfentanil); QT prolongation |
| **Tetracyclines** | 30S | Doxycycline | |
| **Quinolones** | DNA gyrase | Ciprofloxacin | Seizure threshold ↓; QT; tendinopathy; CYP1A2 inhibition (theophylline) |
| **Nitroimidazoles** | DNA damage (anaerobes) | Metronidazole | Disulfiram-like reaction with alcohol |
| **Lincosamides** | 50S | Clindamycin | *C. difficile*; potentiates NMB |
| **Oxazolidinones** | 50S | Linezolid | Weak MAOI (serotonin syndrome) |
| **Antifolates** | Folate synthesis | Trimethoprim, co-trimoxazole | Hyperkalaemia |
| **Antifungals** (azoles) | Ergosterol synthesis | Fluconazole | CYP inhibition |

## Surgical antibiotic prophylaxis

- Give IV **within 60 minutes before incision** (so that tissue levels are adequate at incision); re-dose in long procedures or major blood loss; a single dose is usually enough.
- Choice according to **local policy** (likely organisms, resistance, allergy). Tourniquet surgery: give before inflation.

## Stewardship

Use antibiotics only when needed, choose narrow spectrum where possible, the right dose and duration, review at 48–72 hours ("start smart, then focus"). Resistance: MRSA, ESBL-producing Gram-negatives, carbapenemase producers, *C. difficile* infection after broad-spectrum use.

## Infection prevention

- **Hand hygiene** (the WHO "five moments"), gloves and aprons, aseptic non-touch technique for IV access, full asepsis (hat, mask, gown, gloves, drape) for central lines and neuraxial procedures, single-use equipment, safe sharps disposal.
- **Healthcare-associated infections:** surgical site infection, catheter-related bloodstream infection, ventilator-associated pneumonia, catheter-associated UTI, *C. difficile*. **Prions** (vCJD) resist standard sterilisation: single-use instruments for high-risk tissues.
- **Hospital immunisation:** staff hepatitis B vaccination and immunity checks, influenza and other vaccines per occupational health policy. Needlestick injuries: first aid, report, assess for post-exposure prophylaxis (HIV PEP ideally within hours).
