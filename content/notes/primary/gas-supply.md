## Cylinders

- Molybdenum steel (or aluminium/composite for lightweight cylinders). Test pressures and dates are stamped on the shoulder.
- **UK colour coding** (body white for medical gases, identified by shoulder colour): **oxygen white**; **nitrous oxide blue**; **Entonox blue and white** quarters; **air black and white** quarters; **carbon dioxide grey**; **heliox brown and white**.
- **Pin index safety system** on smaller cylinders (specific pin positions for each gas: oxygen 2–5, N~2~O 3–5, air 1–5, Entonox 7) prevents fitting the wrong cylinder to a yoke. Larger cylinders have bull-nose or handwheel valves. The Bodok seal makes the joint gas-tight.
- **Full pressures:** O~2~ about **137 bar** (newer integral-valve cylinders up to 230 bar); N~2~O about **44–52 bar** (SVP; liquid in cylinder); Entonox 137 bar; air 137 bar.
- **Contents:** an O~2~ cylinder's contents are proportional to pressure. A size E O~2~ cylinder holds about 680 L; a size F about 1360 L (check the label, as newer cylinders vary). At 10 L/min a 680 L cylinder lasts about an hour.

## Measuring pressure: the Bourdon gauge

A coiled, flattened tube of oval cross-section **straightens as internal pressure rises**, moving a pointer through gears. It reads **gauge pressure**, needs no power, and is robust. Aneroid gauges work similarly for lower pressures.

## Pressure regulators

**Pressure-reducing valves** reduce high, falling cylinder pressure to a constant lower pressure (about **4 bar**) for the machine, using a diaphragm and spring. Flow restrictors protect against pipeline pressure surges.

## Pipelines (medical gas pipeline system)

- **Oxygen supply:** usually a **vacuum-insulated evaporator (VIE)** holding liquid oxygen at about −160°C and 5–10 bar, with a back-up cylinder manifold. Oxygen boils off as needed; a superheater warms it before it enters the pipes.
- Delivered at **4 bar** (400 kPa) gauge; **surgical air** at 7 bar (for tools).
- Safety: **Schrader** wall outlets (gas-specific collar indexing), **non-interchangeable screw thread (NIST)** connectors on the machine, colour-coded hoses, tug tests, and **area valve service units (AVSUs)** that isolate zones in fires or leaks. Regular testing and a gas identity check at installation (misconnection has caused deaths).
- **Oxygen concentrators** (zeolite molecular sieves adsorb nitrogen under pressure: pressure swing adsorption) produce about 90–95% oxygen with some argon; used in some hospitals, homes and low-resource settings.
