## Nerve stimulation

- **Supramaximal** stimulus (about 10–20% above the current needed to recruit all fibres; typically 50–60 mA), **square wave**, **short duration (0.1–0.3 ms)**, constant current. Usually at the **ulnar nerve** (adductor pollicis), or the facial nerve (orbicularis oculi or corrugator supercilii) or posterior tibial nerve when the arm is inaccessible.
- Muscles differ in sensitivity: the **diaphragm and laryngeal muscles are more resistant** (recover earlier) than the adductor pollicis; **corrugator supercilii** mirrors the laryngeal muscles (onset); orbicularis oculi may underestimate block.

## Patterns

| Pattern | Description | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Single twitch** | 0.1–1 Hz | Needs a baseline; research |
| **Train-of-four (TOF)** | 4 stimuli at **2 Hz** | **TOF count** (0–4) during deep–moderate block; **TOF ratio** (T4/T1) during recovery: **≥ 0.9 before extubation** |
| **Double burst stimulation (DBS)** | Two short bursts of 50 Hz tetanus 750 ms apart | Fade easier to feel than with TOF (but still unreliable above a ratio of ~0.6) |
| **Tetanic stimulation** | 50 Hz for 5 s | Fade with non-depolarising block; painful; affects later readings at that site for minutes |
| **Post-tetanic count (PTC)** | Single twitches at 1 Hz after 5 s tetanus | **Deep block** when TOF count is 0; a lower PTC means deeper block |

**Depolarising (phase I) block:** reduced but equal responses, no fade, no post-tetanic potentiation. **Non-depolarising block and phase II:** fade and post-tetanic potentiation.

## Quantitative monitors

- **Acceleromyography** (acceleration of the thumb ∝ force), **electromyography** (compound muscle action potential; not affected by movement), **kinemyography**, mechanomyography (the research standard).
- The Association of Anaesthetists (2021) recommends **quantitative monitoring whenever neuromuscular blocking drugs are used**, from induction until recovery is confirmed with a TOF ratio ≥ 0.9.
- Acceleromyography may overestimate the TOF ratio at baseline (normalising to baseline helps).
