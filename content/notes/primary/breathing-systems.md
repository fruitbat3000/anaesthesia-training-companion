## Ideal properties

Low resistance and dead space, efficient use of fresh gas, allows spontaneous and controlled ventilation, humidification, scavenging, lightweight, safe.

## Mapleson classification

| System | Description | FGF to prevent rebreathing (SV) | FGF (IPPV) | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **A (Magill; Lack = coaxial)** | FGF at the bag end, APL valve at the patient end | **~ alveolar minute ventilation (≈ 70–80 mL/kg/min)** — the most efficient for SV | Very inefficient (> 3 × MV) | Lack: expiratory limb inside the inspiratory tube |
| B, C | FGF and APL valve at the patient end | High (1.5–2 × MV) | | C ("Waters") used for resuscitation and transfers |
| **D (Bain = coaxial)** | FGF at the patient end, APL valve at the bag end | **2–3 × MV** (150–200 mL/kg/min) | **~70 mL/kg/min** — efficient for IPPV | Bain: fresh gas in the **inner** tube; check for inner tube disconnection (Pethick test) |
| E (Ayre's T-piece) | No bag or valve | 2–3 × MV | Intermittent occlusion | For children |
| **F (Jackson-Rees)** | T-piece with an open-ended bag | 2–3 × MV (minimum ~3 L/min) | | Children under ~20 kg; the bag shows breathing and allows CPAP |

Memory aid for efficiency in spontaneous breathing: **A > D > C > B** ("All Dogs Can Bite"); for controlled ventilation: **D > B > C > A** ("Dogs Bite Can't Accept").

## The circle system

- Components: fresh gas inlet, **unidirectional inspiratory and expiratory valves**, reservoir bag, APL valve, **CO~2~ absorber** canister, Y-piece.
- Allows **low-flow** anaesthesia (conserves volatile, heat and humidity; reduces pollution and cost). At low flows, inspired concentrations differ from those set: monitor inspired and expired gases.
- **CO~2~ absorbents:** soda lime (mainly calcium hydroxide, with water and small amounts of sodium hydroxide) or lime without strong bases. Reaction: CO~2~ + H~2~O → H~2~CO~3~, then neutralised by hydroxides → carbonates + water + **heat**. An indicator dye changes colour (e.g. white → violet) when exhausted. Granule size 4–8 mesh balances surface area and resistance.
- **Hazards:** exhausted absorbent (rising inspired CO~2~), **compound A** (sevoflurane), **carbon monoxide** (desiccated absorbent with desflurane, historically), fires, valves sticking (rebreathing), leaks and misconnections; nitrogen and other gases accumulate at low flows early in a case.

## Resuscitation devices

**Self-inflating bag–valve–mask** devices (e.g. Ambu) work without a gas supply; with a reservoir and 15 L/min oxygen they deliver about 85–90% oxygen. The non-rebreathing valve prevents exhaled gas returning.
