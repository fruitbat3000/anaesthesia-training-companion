## Antiepileptics

| Drug | Mechanism | Anaesthetic relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Phenytoin | Na^+^ channel block | **Enzyme inducer**; zero-order kinetics; arrhythmias and hypotension with rapid IV; resistance to NMBs with chronic use |
| Carbamazepine | Na^+^ channel block | Enzyme inducer; hyponatraemia |
| Sodium valproate | Na^+^ channels, ↑ GABA | Enzyme inhibitor; thrombocytopenia and platelet dysfunction; teratogenic |
| Levetiracetam | SV2A binding | Few interactions; IV form (useful perioperatively) |
| Lamotrigine | Na^+^ channel block | Rash |
| Gabapentin, pregabalin | α~2~δ subunit of Ca^2+^ channels | Also for neuropathic pain; sedation, respiratory depression with opioids |
| Benzodiazepines | GABA~A~ | First-line in status epilepticus (then levetiracetam, phenytoin or valproate, then anaesthesia) |

Continue antiepileptics perioperatively (omitting doses risks seizures); many anaesthetic drugs lower (propofol, thiopental, benzodiazepines) or occasionally raise (high-dose local anaesthetics, pethidine's metabolite, sevoflurane at high concentration, tramadol) the seizure threshold.

## Antidepressants

- **SSRIs** (sertraline, citalopram, fluoxetine): hyponatraemia (SIADH), platelet dysfunction and bleeding, **serotonin syndrome** with tramadol, pethidine, fentanyl (rarely), methylene blue, ondansetron (rarely), linezolid. Citalopram prolongs QT.
- **Tricyclics** (amitriptyline): anticholinergic, α-blocking, Na^+^ channel blocking; **overdose → wide QRS, arrhythmias, seizures, hypotension** (treat with sodium bicarbonate); interactions with sympathomimetics.
- **MAOIs** (phenelzine, tranylcypromine; moclobemide is reversible): **hypertensive crisis with indirect sympathomimetics** (ephedrine, metaraminol partly) and tyramine; **serotonin syndrome with pethidine and tramadol** (and possibly other serotonergic drugs). Use direct-acting vasopressors in reduced doses; decisions about stopping should be made with psychiatric input.
- **SNRIs** (venlafaxine, duloxetine), mirtazapine.

**Serotonin syndrome:** agitation, confusion, **clonus and hyperreflexia**, tremor, hyperthermia, sweating, tachycardia, diarrhoea. Stop serotonergic drugs; supportive care; benzodiazepines; cyproheptadine; cooling. (Distinguish from neuroleptic malignant syndrome — rigidity, slower onset, dopamine antagonists — and malignant hyperthermia.)

## Antipsychotics and lithium

- **Antipsychotics:** sedation, hypotension (α-blockade), QT prolongation, extrapyramidal effects, **neuroleptic malignant syndrome** (fever, rigidity, autonomic instability, raised CK).
- **Lithium:** narrow therapeutic index; renal excretion (**levels rise with dehydration, NSAIDs, ACE inhibitors and thiazides**); potentiates NMBs; nephrogenic DI, hypothyroidism. Consider withholding 24 h before major surgery and restarting when eating and drinking with normal renal function (with psychiatric input).

## Parkinson's disease drugs

Levodopa (with a dopa-decarboxylase inhibitor), dopamine agonists, MAO-B inhibitors (selegiline, rasagiline), COMT inhibitors. **Do not miss doses** (risk of rigidity, dysphagia, aspiration, neuroleptic malignant-like syndrome): use a nasogastric route or a **rotigotine patch** if nil by mouth. **Avoid metoclopramide, droperidol and haloperidol**; use ondansetron or domperidone.

## CNS stimulants

Amphetamines and methylphenidate (ADHD), modafinil: sympathomimetic, raised anaesthetic requirement acutely; interactions with vasopressors.
