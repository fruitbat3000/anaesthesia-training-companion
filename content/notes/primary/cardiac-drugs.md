## Ischaemic heart disease

**Goal:** improve the oxygen supply–demand balance (reduce heart rate, preload, afterload and contractility; increase coronary flow) and prevent thrombosis.

| Drug | Mechanism | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Nitrates** (GTN, isosorbide mononitrate) | NO donors → cGMP → venodilation (↓ preload, wall stress), coronary dilation | Tolerance with continuous use; headache; hypotension with PDE5 inhibitors (sildenafil) |
| **β-blockers** | ↓ Heart rate and contractility; longer diastole | Continue perioperatively |
| **Calcium channel blockers** | Vasodilation (dihydropyridines); rate control (verapamil, diltiazem) | |
| **Nicorandil** | K~ATP~ opener with nitrate activity | Ulceration (mouth, GI) |
| **Ivabradine** | Blocks I~f~ (funny current) in the SA node → slows rate without negative inotropy | Sinus rhythm only |
| **Ranolazine** | Late Na^+^ current inhibitor | |
| **Antiplatelets** | Aspirin; P2Y~12~ inhibitors (clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor) | **Dual antiplatelet therapy after coronary stents**: do not stop without cardiology advice (stent thrombosis risk) |
| **Statins** | HMG-CoA reductase inhibition; plaque stabilisation | Continue perioperatively |

## Heart failure with reduced ejection fraction

The mainstays that improve survival (NICE NG106, ESC):

- **ACE inhibitor** (or ARB) or **sacubitril/valsartan** (neprilysin inhibitor + ARB: raises natriuretic peptides; vasodilation — expect hypotension);
- **β-blocker** (bisoprolol, carvedilol);
- **mineralocorticoid receptor antagonist** (spironolactone, eplerenone: hyperkalaemia);
- **SGLT2 inhibitor** (dapagliflozin, empagliflozin): risk of **euglycaemic ketoacidosis** perioperatively — usually withheld for a period before surgery according to current guidance;
- **loop diuretics** for congestion; digoxin, ivabradine and devices (CRT, ICD) for selected patients.

## Perioperative implications

- Continue β-blockers and statins; do not start high-dose β-blockers de novo just before surgery.
- Plan for vasodilator-induced hypotension (ACEi/ARB/ARNI) and fluid balance issues (diuretics).
- Check potassium and renal function.
- For patients with stents or recent MI, timing of elective surgery and antiplatelet management need cardiology and surgical agreement (ESC 2022 guidance).
