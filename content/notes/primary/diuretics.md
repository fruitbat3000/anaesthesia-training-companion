| Class | Site | Mechanism | Uses | Effects and side effects |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Carbonic anhydrase inhibitors** (acetazolamide) | Proximal tubule | ↓ HCO~3~^−^ reabsorption | Glaucoma, altitude sickness, metabolic alkalosis, idiopathic intracranial hypertension | **Hyperchloraemic metabolic acidosis**, hypokalaemia |
| **Osmotic** (mannitol) | Proximal tubule and descending limb (water-permeable segments) | Filtered but not reabsorbed; holds water in the tubule | Raised ICP, (historically) renal protection | Initial plasma volume expansion (heart failure), then hypovolaemia, hypernatraemia; crosses a damaged BBB |
| **Loop** (furosemide, bumetanide) | **Thick ascending limb** | Block **Na^+^/K^+^/2Cl^−^** co-transporter | Heart failure, oedema, hyperkalaemia, hypercalcaemia | Most potent; **hypokalaemia**, hypomagnesaemia, **metabolic alkalosis**, hyponatraemia, hypocalcaemia, **ototoxicity** (with aminoglycosides) |
| **Thiazides** (bendroflumethiazide, indapamide) | Distal convoluted tubule | Block **Na^+^/Cl^−^** co-transporter | Hypertension, oedema, nephrogenic DI (paradoxically) | Hypokalaemia, **hyponatraemia**, **hypercalcaemia**, hyperglycaemia, hyperuricaemia (gout) |
| **Potassium-sparing: aldosterone antagonists** (spironolactone, eplerenone) | Collecting duct | Mineralocorticoid receptor antagonism | Heart failure, cirrhosis, hyperaldosteronism | **Hyperkalaemia**, gynaecomastia (spironolactone) |
| **Potassium-sparing: ENaC blockers** (amiloride, triamterene) | Collecting duct | Block epithelial Na^+^ channels | With thiazides or loops to limit K^+^ loss | Hyperkalaemia |
| **SGLT2 inhibitors** (dapagliflozin) | Proximal tubule | Glycosuria and natriuresis | Diabetes, heart failure, CKD | Euglycaemic ketoacidosis, genital infections |

**Why loop and thiazide diuretics cause hypokalaemia:** more Na^+^ and fluid reach the collecting duct, where Na^+^ reabsorption through ENaC drives K^+^ secretion, amplified by the secondary aldosterone rise.

> [!exam] In the exam
> - **AKT:** match each diuretic to its site and transporter; electrolyte effects (especially calcium with loop versus thiazide).
> - **CASE:** a patient on furosemide and spironolactone with a potassium of 6.1 mmol/L before surgery.
