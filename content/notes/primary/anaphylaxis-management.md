## Recognition

Sudden **hypotension** (the commonest presenting feature), tachycardia or bradycardia, **bronchospasm** (high airway pressure, falling or sloping ETCO~2~), desaturation, angioedema, urticaria or flushing (often absent in severe cases), and cardiac arrest. Consider it whenever there is unexplained collapse after drug administration (antibiotics, neuromuscular blockers, chlorhexidine, patent blue dye, colloids, latex).

## Immediate management (follow the QRH and RCUK guidance)

1. **Call for help**; declare anaphylaxis; note the time.
2. **Remove likely triggers**: stop drug or colloid infusions, remove chlorhexidine-containing items; maintain anaesthesia with a volatile if an IV agent may be the trigger (or vice versa).
3. **Airway and oxygen:** 100% O~2~; secure the airway if needed.
4. **Adrenaline:** IV titrated boluses in the monitored anaesthetised adult (for example 50 µg, repeated as needed), or IM (0.5 mg in adults) if no IV access. Start an **adrenaline infusion** if repeated boluses are needed. In children, use weight-based doses.
5. **Fluids:** rapid crystalloid boluses (large volumes may be needed).
6. **Cardiac arrest:** start CPR and follow ALS; adrenaline per the ALS algorithm.
7. **Refractory hypotension:** add other vasopressors (noradrenaline, **vasopressin**, metaraminol), glucagon for patients on β-blockers, consider ECMO where available.
8. **Bronchospasm:** salbutamol, volatile, magnesium.
9. **Second-line drugs** (antihistamines, steroids) are not life-saving and should not delay adrenaline.

## After the event

- **Tryptase** samples (as soon as possible after resuscitation, at 1–2 hours, and at 24 hours or later).
- Admit to a critical care area as needed; biphasic reactions can occur.
- **Refer to a specialist allergy clinic** (the anaesthetist is responsible for the referral), **inform the patient** (in writing), their GP and surgeon, document clearly in the notes, and **report to the MHRA** (Yellow Card) and locally.
- Future anaesthetics: avoid all possible triggers until investigated.
