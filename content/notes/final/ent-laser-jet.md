## Surgery on or below the vocal cords

| Technique | Features | Concerns |
|---|---|---|
| **Microlaryngeal tube** (e.g. 5.0–6.0 mm ID) | Secure airway, cuffed, capnography | Obscures the posterior glottis |
| **Jet ventilation** (supraglottic, subglottic or transtracheal; manual or high-frequency) | Tubeless, good surgical view | **Barotrauma and pneumothorax** if outflow is obstructed; no end-tidal monitoring; aspiration; needs TIVA |
| **HFNO/apnoeic oxygenation (e.g. THRIVE)** | Tubeless for short cases | CO~2~ rises (about 0.15–0.2 kPa/min) — limits duration; not with laser (FiO~2~ high) |
| Intermittent apnoea | Remove tube, operate, re-intubate | Repeated instrumentation |

**Laser surgery:** laser-resistant tubes with saline-filled cuffs, FiO~2~ ≤ 0.3, no N~2~O, eye protection, an airway fire plan.

## Major head and neck cancer surgery

- Patients often have **smoking and alcohol**-related disease (COPD, IHD, malnutrition, alcohol withdrawal), previous radiotherapy (fibrosis, difficult airway), and tumours that distort the airway.
- Airway plan (ATI, elective tracheostomy), long procedures (temperature, pressure areas, fluid balance), **free flap** considerations (see free flap surgery), and postoperative critical care.

## Middle ear and nasal surgery

- **Middle ear:** a bloodless field (moderate controlled hypotension, head-up tilt, **TIVA with remifentanil** is popular), **avoid N~2~O** (pressure changes in the middle ear displace grafts), facial nerve monitoring (avoid neuromuscular block after intubation), PONV prophylaxis (high risk).
- **Nasal and sinus surgery:** vasoconstrictors (cocaine, adrenaline, xylometazoline — watch for hypertension and arrhythmias), throat packs, reinforced LMA or oral RAE tube, smooth emergence.
- **Supraglottic airways for IPPV or SV:** second-generation SGAs are widely used for ENT with surgical agreement; limitations include displacement by gags and leaks.
