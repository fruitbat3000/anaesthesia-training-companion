## Risk-scoring systems

| Tool | Predicts | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| ASA | General fitness | Simple, subjective, not a risk calculator |
| **RCRI (Lee)** | Major cardiac complications after non-cardiac surgery | Six factors; underestimates risk in vascular surgery |
| **P-POSSUM** | Mortality and morbidity | Needs intraoperative variables; overpredicts in low-risk patients |
| **SORT** | 30-day mortality | Preoperative variables only (UK-derived) |
| **NELA score** | Emergency laparotomy mortality | Used for NELA standards |
| **ACS NSQIP** calculator | Procedure-specific outcomes | US-derived |
| Frailty scores (CFS) | Adverse outcomes in older patients | |

Limitations: population estimates applied to individuals, calibration drift, and poor capture of patient-centred outcomes (disability, quality of life). Use them to **inform** decisions, allocate resources (critical care, seniority), and consent — not to decide alone.

## Cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET)

- Incremental exercise on a cycle ergometer with breath-by-breath gas analysis and ECG.
- **Anaerobic threshold (AT)** (the point where anaerobic metabolism supplements aerobic, detected by the V-slope method): an AT below about **10–11 mL/kg/min** is associated with higher perioperative risk for major abdominal surgery. **Peak VO~2~** below about **15 mL/kg/min** likewise. Also ventilatory equivalents for CO~2~ (VE/VCO~2~ > about 34 indicates poor ventilatory efficiency, e.g. pulmonary vascular disease), ECG ischaemia and blood pressure response.
- Uses: risk stratification, identifying unexpected pathology, guiding prehabilitation and level of postoperative care, shared decision-making.

## Other functional assessments

Self-reported exercise tolerance (METs; DASI questionnaire), shuttle walk tests, six-minute walk test, BNP/NT-proBNP (ESC 2022 recommends in some patients), echocardiography for specific indications (murmurs, dyspnoea).

## Prehabilitation and optimisation

**Exercise** (aerobic and resistance), **nutrition** (protein), **psychological** support, **smoking and alcohol** cessation, treatment of **anaemia** (IV iron), diabetes and hypertension optimisation. Multimodal prehabilitation over weeks can improve functional capacity; evidence for outcomes is growing.

## Shared decision-making

A collaborative process (e.g. **BRAN**: Benefits, Risks, Alternatives, and doing Nothing) that combines clinical evidence with what matters to the patient. Supported by decision aids, time, and multidisciplinary input (surgeon, anaesthetist, geriatrician). Particularly important for high-risk and older patients.
