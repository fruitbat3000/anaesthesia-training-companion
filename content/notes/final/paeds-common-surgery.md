## Day surgery in children

Most paediatric surgery is day case: fitness, a suitable home, and parents who understand the instructions. Exclusions include some ex-premature infants, significant comorbidity, OSA needing overnight monitoring (e.g. adenotonsillectomy in severe OSA or young age), and difficult social circumstances.

## Tonsillectomy

OSA versus recurrent infection (OSA children are more sensitive to opioids and at risk of postoperative respiratory events); shared airway; analgesia (paracetamol, ibuprofen, dexamethasone, careful morphine — **no codeine in under-18s**); PONV prophylaxis; bleeding (see the bleeding tonsil).

## Fluids

- **Deficit:** assess dehydration clinically (mild < 5%, moderate 5–10%, severe > 10%); replace over 24–48 h (faster for shock).
- **Resuscitation:** isotonic crystalloid **10 mL/kg** boluses with reassessment (NICE NG29; 20 mL/kg is used in some settings).
- **Maintenance:** isotonic fluids (e.g. 0.9% saline with 5% glucose, or balanced solutions) using the 4-2-1 rule, often **restricted to about two-thirds** postoperatively because of ADH release; check electrolytes daily. **Avoid hypotonic fluids** (hyponatraemic encephalopathy).
- **Intraoperative:** replace losses with balanced crystalloid; glucose for infants and at-risk children.

## Blood

- **Estimated blood volume:** preterm ~90–100 mL/kg, term neonate ~85–90 mL/kg, infant ~80 mL/kg, child ~75 mL/kg.
- **Allowable blood loss** ≈ EBV × (starting Hct − target Hct) / average Hct.
- Transfusion volume (mL) ≈ weight (kg) × desired rise in Hb (g/L) × about 0.4–0.5 (e.g. 4–5 mL/kg per 10 g/L; follow local formulae). Products in mL/kg (FFP 10–15 mL/kg, platelets 10–15 mL/kg, cryoprecipitate 5–10 mL/kg).

## TIVA in children

Paediatric models (e.g. Kataria, Paedfusor; Eleveld covers a wide age range) for propofol; larger doses per kg than adults; injection pain; watch for propofol infusion syndrome with long, high-dose infusions.

## Regional anaesthesia in children

Usually performed under general anaesthesia (accepted practice). **Caudal** (levobupivacaine 0.25% 0.5–1 mL/kg; additives such as clonidine prolong it), penile block, ilioinguinal/iliohypogastric, rectus sheath, TAP, and peripheral limb blocks under ultrasound; epidurals and spinals in specialist centres. Doses by weight within limits; test dose with adrenaline (T-wave changes).
