## Principles

The **same standards** of equipment, monitoring, assistance and recovery as theatre, with extra planning: an unfamiliar environment, limited space and access, darkness, lack of skilled help, distance from theatres, and patients who may be sick or uncooperative. Check the equipment, oxygen supply, suction, drugs, communication and escape routes before starting.

## MRI

- **Hazards:** the static magnetic field (projectiles, displacement of implants, device malfunction), gradient fields (noise, peripheral nerve stimulation), radiofrequency energy (heating and **burns** from looped cables and ECG leads), quenching of helium (asphyxia).
- **Zones** with controlled access; screening of patients and staff; **MR-safe or MR-conditional** anaesthetic machines, monitors and pumps (or long lines through waveguides).
- Monitoring is often degraded (ECG artefact); capnography with long sampling lines (delay).
- Children and patients who cannot lie still often need GA or deep sedation; plan recovery nearby.

## Electroconvulsive therapy (ECT)

- Treatment for severe depression and other psychiatric conditions; a generalised seizure is induced under GA with muscle relaxation.
- **Physiology:** initial **parasympathetic** response (bradycardia, even asystole) followed by a **sympathetic** surge (tachycardia, hypertension) — risky in cardiovascular disease; raised ICP and IOP; myalgia.
- **Anaesthesia:** a short GA (propofol, methohexital, etomidate or ketamine — propofol shortens seizure duration; etomidate and ketamine lengthen it), **suxamethonium** (modified seizure; a tourniquet on one arm can show the seizure), hyperventilation (lowers seizure threshold), bite block, and treatments repeated often (twice weekly courses).
- **Consent and capacity:** ECT may be given to patients lacking capacity under the Mental Health Act and Mental Capacity Act with specific safeguards; respect valid advance refusals.

## Other remote sites

- **Endoscopy** (ERCP in the prone position, shared airway; propofol sedation by anaesthetists for complex cases).
- **Cardiology** (cardioversion — brief anaesthesia with a full stomach risk assessment and anticoagulation checked; EP studies; TAVI).
- **Interventional radiology** (embolisation, TIPSS, thrombectomy).
- **Emergency department:** RSI for sick patients with limited information, then transfer.
- **Dental sedation and GA** (often for children and adults with learning disabilities; consent and capacity; shared airway).
