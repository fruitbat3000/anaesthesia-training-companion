## Cardiac output and goal-directed therapy

- **Devices:** calibrated pulse contour (PiCCO: transpulmonary thermodilution; LiDCO: lithium), uncalibrated pulse contour, **oesophageal Doppler**, and the pulmonary artery catheter (thermodilution, pulmonary artery pressures, mixed venous saturation). See the Primary note on measuring cardiac output.
- **Goal-directed therapy (GDT):** protocolised fluid and inotrope administration to optimise stroke volume or oxygen delivery (e.g. giving 250 mL boluses until stroke volume no longer rises by 10%). Evidence of benefit is mixed in modern enhanced recovery pathways but it can help in high-risk surgery.
- **Dynamic indices:** stroke volume and pulse pressure variation predict fluid responsiveness in patients who are ventilated with tidal volumes ≥ 8 mL/kg, in sinus rhythm, with a closed chest.

## Transoesophageal echocardiography (TOE)

Uses in theatre: cardiac surgery (valve assessment, de-airing, ventricular function), unexplained haemodynamic instability (**rescue TOE**: hypovolaemia, LV/RV failure, tamponade, dynamic LVOT obstruction, PE), and guiding structural procedures. Contraindications: oesophageal disease (strictures, varices, recent surgery). Complications: oropharyngeal and oesophageal injury.

## Pacing

- **Indications for temporary pacing:** symptomatic bradycardia unresponsive to drugs, complete heart block, Mobitz II, some drug toxicities, after cardiac surgery.
- **Advanced functions:** rate response, cardiac resynchronisation therapy (biventricular pacing for heart failure with LBBB), ICDs (antitachycardia pacing and shocks), leadless pacemakers.

## Electrophysiology procedures

- **AF ablation** (often under GA to keep the patient still and to control ventilation; heparinised; risk of tamponade, oesophageal injury, stroke) and **VT ablation** (sick patients with poor LV function; arrhythmia induction; may need haemodynamic support).
- **ICD insertion and testing** (defibrillation threshold testing is less common now): deep sedation or GA during induced VF.
- Remote location, limited access to the patient, fluoroscopy, long procedures and anticoagulation.

> - **AKT:** limitations of dynamic indices; rescue TOE findings; CRT.
