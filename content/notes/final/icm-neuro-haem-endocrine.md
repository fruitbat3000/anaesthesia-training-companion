## Neurology

- **Sedation and delirium:** analgesia-first light sedation (RASS 0 to −2), daily sedation holds, CAM-ICU screening, non-drug delirium measures, early mobilisation; avoid benzodiazepine infusions where possible. Dexmedetomidine and propofol are common.
- **Reduced consciousness:** structural (stroke, haemorrhage, trauma), metabolic (glucose, sodium, hepatic, uraemic, hypercapnia), infective (meningitis, encephalitis), toxic, seizures (non-convulsive status — EEG).
- **Neuromuscular weakness:** **Guillain–Barré** (ascending weakness, areflexia, autonomic instability; monitor vital capacity — intubate if < about 15–20 mL/kg or rapidly falling; IV immunoglobulin or plasma exchange) and **myasthenic crisis** (IVIG, plasma exchange, ventilatory support). ICU-acquired weakness (critical illness polyneuropathy and myopathy).
- **Hypoxic brain injury** after cardiac arrest: temperature control, avoid hypotension and hypoxia, multimodal prognostication after at least 72 hours; **brainstem death** testing when appropriate.
- **Autonomic disorders:** dysautonomia in GBS, spinal cord injury and tetanus.

## Haematology

- **Anaemia** (restrictive transfusion), **thrombocytopenia** (sepsis, DIC, heparin-induced thrombocytopenia — use the 4Ts score, drugs, TTP — plasma exchange), **coagulopathy** (DIC, liver disease, anticoagulants, dilution), **haematological malignancy** (neutropenic sepsis, tumour lysis syndrome, hyperviscosity), sickle cell crises (acute chest syndrome).
- **Blood-borne viruses:** hepatitis B and C, HIV — universal precautions, needlestick management and post-exposure prophylaxis, and the effects of HIV and antiretrovirals (drug interactions, e.g. ritonavir inhibits CYP3A4) on anaesthesia and ICU care.

## Endocrine emergencies

DKA, HHS, diabetes insipidus and SIADH, adrenal crisis, thyroid storm and myxoedema coma, hypercalcaemia and hypocalcaemia, hypoglycaemia (see endocrine notes).

## Children in intensive care

Recognition and initial management of the critically ill child (sepsis, bronchiolitis, trauma, seizures, DKA), with early involvement of paediatric retrieval services (see complex paediatrics).
