## The three legs of assessment

1. **Respiratory mechanics:** FEV~1~ (and predicted postoperative FEV~1~, ppoFEV~1~).
2. **Lung parenchymal function:** diffusing capacity (DLCO) and ppoDLCO; arterial blood gases.
3. **Cardiopulmonary reserve:** exercise testing (stair climbing, shuttle walk, **CPET** with VO~2~ max).

## Predicted postoperative values

- **ppoFEV~1~ = preoperative FEV~1~ × (1 − number of functional segments removed / total functional segments)** (19 segments in total, or adjusted for obstructed segments; perfusion scans help for pneumonectomy).
- Example: FEV~1~ 70% predicted, right lower lobe (5 segments) removed → ppoFEV~1~ ≈ 70 × (14/19) ≈ 52%.
- **ppoFEV~1~ and ppoDLCO > 60% predicted**: low risk. Values between about **30% and 60%** need exercise testing. Below about **30%** (either): high risk — consider limited resection or non-surgical treatment.

## Exercise testing

- **VO~2~ max > 20 mL/kg/min:** low risk. **< 10 mL/kg/min:** high risk for major resection. Intermediate values need individualised decisions.
- Simple tests: **stair climbing** (ability to climb > 22 m, about 5–6 flights, suggests low risk), incremental shuttle walk (> 400 m).

## Other factors

Cardiac risk (e.g. thoracic RCRI), age and frailty, smoking cessation, nutritional status, pulmonary rehabilitation (prehabilitation), and the patient's own priorities in a shared decision-making discussion.
