## Choosing an approach

| Surgery | Block | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Shoulder, clavicle (lateral), proximal humerus | **Interscalene** (C5–6) | Near-certain hemidiaphragmatic paresis at usual volumes; alternatives: **superior trunk block**, suprascapular (+ axillary) nerve blocks, low-volume techniques |
| Humerus, elbow, forearm, hand | **Supraclavicular** ("the spinal of the arm") | Dense and fast; pneumothorax risk low with ultrasound; phrenic block possible |
| Elbow, forearm, hand | **Infraclavicular** (cords around the axillary artery, deep to pectoralis) | Good for catheters; deep and angled |
| Forearm and hand | **Axillary** | Separate musculocutaneous injection; safest in coagulopathy (compressible) |
| Hand (rescue) | Forearm/elbow blocks of median, ulnar, radial nerves | |

## Pharmacology

- Choice of agent by duration: lidocaine or prilocaine (short), levobupivacaine or ropivacaine (long).
- **Adjuvants:** **dexamethasone** (perineural or IV, prolongs analgesia; IV is similarly effective and avoids perineural use off-label), clonidine, dexmedetomidine; adrenaline (marks intravascular injection; limits absorption). Opioids add little to peripheral blocks.
- **Catheters** extend analgesia (e.g. interscalene for major shoulder surgery).

## Getting a good ultrasound image

- **Probe choice** (high-frequency linear for most upper limb blocks), **depth** (target in the middle of the screen), **gain** (uniform brightness), **focus** at the target, **PART** manoeuvres (pressure, alignment, rotation, tilt) to improve nerve visibility (anisotropy), and colour Doppler to identify vessels.
- Artefacts: acoustic enhancement deep to arteries (can mimic nerves), shadowing, reverberation from the needle.

## Safety

Sterile technique; monitoring; **injection pressure monitoring** (avoid > 15 psi); hydrodissection; the needle tip seen at all times; incremental injection with aspiration; **Stop Before You Block**; checking coagulation for deep or non-compressible blocks; documentation; limb protection advice; follow-up of neurological symptoms.
