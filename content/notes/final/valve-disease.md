## Assessment

Symptoms (angina, syncope, breathlessness), functional capacity, examination (murmurs, signs of heart failure), **echocardiography** (valve area, gradients, ventricular function, pulmonary pressures), and the timing of any valve intervention relative to non-cardiac surgery (the ESC 2022 guideline recommends intervention before elective surgery for symptomatic severe AS where feasible).

## Haemodynamic goals

| Lesion | Heart rate | Rhythm | Preload | Afterload (SVR) | Contractility |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Aortic stenosis** | **Low–normal** (60–80) | **Sinus** (atrial kick vital) | Maintain | **Maintain** (avoid falls) | Maintain |
| **Mitral stenosis** | **Low** (maximise filling time) | Sinus if possible; rate-control AF | Maintain | Maintain; avoid ↑ PVR (hypoxia, hypercapnia, acidosis) | Maintain |
| **Aortic regurgitation** | **Normal–high** (less diastolic time for regurgitation) | | Maintain | **Reduce** (forward flow) | Maintain |
| **Mitral regurgitation** | Normal–high | | Maintain | **Reduce** | Maintain |

## Aortic stenosis

- Severe AS: **valve area < 1.0 cm^2^**, **mean gradient > 40 mmHg**, peak velocity > 4 m/s (beware low-flow, low-gradient AS with poor LV function).
- The hypertrophied LV has a **fixed stroke volume**, high oxygen demand and depends on diastolic coronary perfusion. **Hypotension → reduced coronary perfusion → ischaemia → further hypotension** (a downward spiral). Treat hypotension promptly with a **vasoconstrictor** (phenylephrine or metaraminol); avoid tachycardia.
- Neuraxial anaesthesia is not absolutely contraindicated but sudden sympathectomy is dangerous: titrated epidural or low-dose spinal with invasive monitoring, or a carefully titrated GA.

## TAVI and other structural procedures

**Transcatheter aortic valve implantation** (usually transfemoral, often under local anaesthesia and sedation) for patients at higher surgical risk or older age. Complications: vascular injury, stroke, heart block (pacing), paravalvular leak, annular rupture, coronary occlusion, tamponade. **Rapid ventricular pacing** reduces cardiac output during deployment.

## Infective endocarditis prophylaxis

In the UK (NICE), antibiotic prophylaxis against endocarditis is **not routinely recommended** for dental or other procedures; for patients at high risk (prosthetic valves, previous endocarditis, some congenital heart disease) undergoing procedures at an infected site, antibiotics that cover the likely organisms are given. Follow current national and local guidance.
