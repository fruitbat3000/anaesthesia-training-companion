## Diabetes (Stage 2 emphasis)

Complications that matter: cardiovascular disease (silent ischaemia), nephropathy, **autonomic neuropathy** (gastroparesis → aspiration; labile BP; painless ischaemia), stiff joints (difficult laryngoscopy), infection and poor healing. See the Primary diabetes notes for perioperative glucose management.

## Thyroid

- **Hyperthyroidism:** make euthyroid before elective surgery (carbimazole or PTU, β-blockade); **thyroid storm** (fever, tachycardia, AF, agitation, heart failure) — β-blockers, antithyroid drugs, iodide, hydrocortisone, cooling, supportive care.
- **Hypothyroidism:** sensitivity to anaesthetics, hypothermia, hyponatraemia, hypoventilation; **myxoedema coma** (a medical emergency).
- **Thyroid surgery:** goitre with **tracheal compression or retrosternal extension** (CT; flow–volume loop; plan awake or asleep techniques; rarely need cardiopulmonary bypass standby), recurrent laryngeal nerve monitoring (NIM tubes: avoid long-acting NMB), postoperative haematoma (open the wound), hypocalcaemia, tracheomalacia (rare).

## Adrenal

- **Adrenal insufficiency** (primary or secondary including exogenous steroids): perioperative steroid cover (2020 guideline); crisis presents with refractory hypotension → hydrocortisone.
- **Cushing's syndrome:** hypertension, diabetes, obesity, OSA, fragile skin and veins, osteoporosis, poor healing, hypokalaemia.
- **Conn's syndrome** (hyperaldosteronism): hypertension, hypokalaemia (correct before surgery; spironolactone).
- **Phaeochromocytoma:** see the separate note.

## Pituitary

**Acromegaly** (difficult airway: macroglossia, prognathism, laryngeal narrowing; OSA; cardiomyopathy; diabetes), **Cushing's disease**, prolactinoma, and **hypopituitarism** (steroid and thyroid replacement; DI after surgery).

## Endocrine emergencies in critical care

DKA and HHS (see diabetes), **diabetes insipidus** (after head injury or brainstem death: polyuria, hypernatraemia — desmopressin), **SIADH**, thyroid storm, myxoedema coma, adrenal crisis, hypercalcaemia (malignancy; fluids, bisphosphonates), and hypoglycaemia.

> [!exam] In the exam
> - **AKT:** thyroid storm treatment; steroid cover; acromegaly airway issues.
> - **FCPE:** a patient with a large retrosternal goitre and orthopnoea for thyroidectomy: plan with the surgeon.
