## Sickle cell disease

- HbSS (sickle cell anaemia) and compound states (HbSC). Chronic haemolysis, vaso-occlusive crises, acute chest syndrome, stroke, splenic infarction, renal and pulmonary complications.
- **Perioperative care:** involve haematology; **preoperative transfusion** to Hb about 100 g/L for medium- and high-risk surgery in HbSS (per evidence from trials); avoid **hypoxia, dehydration, hypothermia, acidosis and stasis** (tourniquets are relatively contraindicated); good analgesia; incentive spirometry and early mobilisation to prevent **acute chest syndrome**.
- **Sickle cell trait** is generally not a major anaesthetic problem.

## Bleeding and clotting disorders

Haemophilia (factor replacement with haematology), von Willebrand disease (desmopressin or concentrates), thrombocytopenia (ITP, drugs), **thrombophilias** (VTE prophylaxis), **antiphospholipid syndrome** (thrombosis despite prolonged APTT in vitro), heparin-induced thrombocytopenia.

## Cancer therapy

- **Chemotherapy:** cardiotoxicity (**anthracyclines** such as doxorubicin: cardiomyopathy; trastuzumab), pulmonary toxicity (**bleomycin**: sensitivity to high FiO~2~ — use the lowest FiO~2~ needed), neuropathy (vincristine, platins), nephrotoxicity (cisplatin), bone marrow suppression (infection, bleeding), mucositis.
- **Radiotherapy:** fibrosis of the neck (difficult airway and FONA), lungs and heart.
- **Immunotherapies** (checkpoint inhibitors): endocrine and other autoimmune effects (adrenal insufficiency, thyroiditis, pneumonitis).
- Tumour effects: anaemia, hypercalcaemia, SVC obstruction, mediastinal masses, cachexia.

## Nutrition

Assess with weight loss, BMI, intake and screening tools (MUST), albumin (a marker of illness rather than nutrition). Malnutrition impairs wound healing, immunity, respiratory muscle function and thermoregulation. Prehabilitation includes nutritional support; perioperative feeding (early enteral), and refeeding precautions.

## Cultural, ethnic and personal factors

Respect beliefs about blood products, animal-derived products (e.g. porcine heparin, gelatin in colloids or capsules), fasting during religious periods, and end-of-life care. **Ethnicity** matters for some conditions (sickle cell, G6PD deficiency, CYP2D6 variants), pulse oximetry bias with darker skin, and inequalities in outcomes (MBRRACE). Use professional interpreters.

## Substance misuse and addiction

Opioid dependence (continue methadone or buprenorphine; higher analgesic needs; regional anaesthesia), alcohol (withdrawal prophylaxis, thiamine, liver disease), stimulants (cardiovascular risk), and the wider psychosocial context; involve addiction services.
